title: Project Viikate
description: During my exchange programme in Aalto University, I had the privilege of joining Project Viikate, a student-led project to develop an aerodynamically-controlled actively-guided rocket. <br> Our avionics team was in charge of designing and developing the flight computer with a Teensy 4.1 microcontroller. I was responsible for writing parts of the software to extract flight data from our IMU, magnetometer and barometer, as well as implementing a Kalman filter for smoothing and processing flight data. I also helped with assembly and troubleshooting of the flight computer. <br> The rocket was launched in May 2026, with partial success. Unfortunately, some guidance issues occurred due state estimation algorithm issues.
