title: Bernina Design Challenge
description: The Bernina Design Challenge gave us the opportunity to work with BERNINA machines and their software solutions, as well as to design interactive AR experiences with Reality Composer. <br> In line with our focus area of Sustainable Design & Material Innovation, we conceptualised ReStore, a storage solution for makerspaces to recycle their 3d printed waste. <br> 3D printed waste was pressed with a heat press (with many iterations to find ideal settings), cut to size, and assembled with the help of BERNINA machines.

skills: 
- BERNINA machines and software (Bianco)
- Blender
- AR (Reality Composer)
