title: Open House 2027 door gift (Design and Manufacturing project) (In Progress)
description: We were instructed to design and manufacture a door gift meant for Open House 2027, with an instructed MOQ of 1000 and a target cost of less than $10 per gift. <br> Inspired by the shape of SUTD's buildings 1 and 2, we decided to design a 3D maze with an FDM 3D printed interior, a thermoformed shell and a waterjet cut aluminium base. <br> Further developments are being made to improve the design of the maze and the thermoformed exterior shell, as well as for final analysis of cost and assembly.

skills: 
- CAD (Onshape)
- Water Jet Cutting
- Thermoforming
- FDM
- DFMA
