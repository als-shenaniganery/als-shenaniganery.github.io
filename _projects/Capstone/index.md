title: Actively Actuated Ultrasound Phantom with Digital Twin (Capstone Project) (In Progress)
description: Our capstone project is centered around designing and fabricating an acoustically accurate leg phantom to validate PreciX's GATOR Pro device. <br> PreciX's GATOR Pro device combines data from ultrasound and IMUs to better diagnose ACL tears and their severity. They require a controlled, repeatable and configurable testing rig that is able to output the Joint Coordinate System (JCS) representation of knee movement. <br> For the GATOR Pro to track the underlying femoral bone surface accurately, the tissue-mimicking material (TMM) must reproduce the acoustic behaviour of the muscle surrounding the bone. Molds also needed to be designed for each muscle group in the leg, with casting methodology to be developed.
